# T08 — Reactions, activity, and settings

## Objective

Complete the supporting product surfaces and make feedback feel delightful.

## Acceptance criteria

- Show Text, shockwave, and random reactions render cleanly and respect Reduce Motion.
- Activity displays recent pattern, action, outcome, and time with a bounded history.
- Settings covers sensitivity, timing, mode, feedback, and reset.
- Empty, permission-error, and first-use states are polished.
- All preferences persist locally.
